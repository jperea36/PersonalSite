ROCKSTAR DIABETIC: Blood Sugar 800 - Stop Feeding the Demon Review

by C.C. Rowland · Biography · Read September 2026

★★★☆☆

Rockstar Diabetic is a biography based on a popular artist reliving his glory days until he's hit with the harsh reality of diabetes and his life is turned upside down.

The book was a quick read with a lot of content intended to be meaningful. The author talks reminiscingly about his glory days constantly out binging on drugs and alcohol that is intended to give the reader a personalized background of the author. His use of allegory and metaphors made it interesting along with linking the frequent use back to his grandfather that did the same. Additionally, I thought it showed good growth for the author when he was smacked by reality and forced to face the cards he had been dealt. The main issues I had that keep this from reaching a higher rating is that the author frequented back to using metaphors which made the points he was attempting to make muddled. The plot was hard to focus on because their writing style had the story jumping backwards, forwards, and every direction only to repeatedly jump around again and again. One moment he's reliving his glory days, the next he's going into a deep medical confrontation, then he will randomly jump to family. I found that reading through this meant I was continuing to question where in the timeline he was referencing and the jump between story telling to stating medical facts made the story awkward and felt like he didn't incorporate that medical knowledge well into the story, moreso that he just stated them to show how much he learned. The information is accurate for diabetes and he is well versed in the medical terminology for it, but that didn't help in the telling of his story.

Close with who you'd recommend it to, or where it lands compared to similar books. It's still an interesting story despite the flaws stated above and seeing how a rockstart went from fame and drugs to struggling through medical issues and relationship issues with family and his wife. I would recommend this book to anyone who might be interested in rockstars/celebrities and biographies.
