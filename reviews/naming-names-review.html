Naming Names: A Blacklist Murder Mystery

by Stuart Greenberg · Fiction/Mystery Thriller · Read September 2026

★★★★☆

Naming Names is a fictional murder mystery tail enveloping the dark side of Hollywood and politics. The premise of the story is about a killer on the loose in Hollywood and a detective that chases after the killer. The detective is blocked from doing work by their chief and by the upper echelon of politics up to a corrupt senator. It's a thrilling tail of misdirection and playing into roles to get a job done. Corruption steeps over the people who run Hollywood, the mayor, the chief of police, and a senator. It plays into the classic theme of patriotism versus communism as a background cover for the whole case.

The story was written well using performative expression to give an enriched experience while reading. The author excellently gave in depth details painting a very clear picture of the story as it goes on. It flowed well, the timeline was easy to follow as it is very linear. The characters stayed in focus with minimal perspective swapping between only a handful of characters to give you every side of the story. It was an exciting story to read and gave rise to emotions that had me cheering on the detective. The parts the story starts to fall short, thus dropping it down to 4/5 stars, were towards the conclusion of the story due to an abrupt ending that left me feeling unsatisfied. Albeit the epilogue closes off the story more, I felt I was left hanging as all the detail that came crashing down into the end, was quickly closed out with no explicit detail about the end of the case.

Regardless, this is a classic story of detectives versus murderer that made for an entertaining read. I would recommend this to fans of myster thrillers and detective books. I would say it holds similarities to crime shows like CSI and it's similar to the type of deductive reasoning you might see in Sherlock Holmes. There isn't much action in this book though, so fans of action thrillers might not be as interested with the heavy focus on detective work.
